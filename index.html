FEATURED · MATHEMATICAL RESEARCH

Equilibrium of a Stochastic Economic System

Study of the existence and uniqueness of a general equilibrium within a random economic system at the Institut Camille Jordan. Mathematical proof of equilibrium in expectation under continuous distributions using Brouwer's Fixed Point Theorem. Implemented Monte-Carlo simulations and fixed-point algorithms to estimate expected supply and demand and empirically evaluate the law of value.

Python Stochastic Economics Monte Carlo Brouwer's Fixed Point
GitHub Report
FEATURED · QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

Portfolio Optimization via Neural Networks

Comparison of classical econometric models (BEKK-GJR) with neural architectures (MLP, LSTM) for conditional volatility forecasting. Developed a multivariate LSTM architecture (MLSTM) predicting the conditional covariance matrix through a Cholesky decomposition to guarantee positive definiteness. Designed Minimum-Variance, Risk-Parity, and Sharpe-optimal portfolio strategies incorporating proportional transaction costs using OSQP and CVXPY.

PyTorch BEKK-GJR MLSTM Cholesky CVXPY Portfolio Optimization
