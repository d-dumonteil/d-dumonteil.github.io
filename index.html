Portfolio Optimization with Volatility Models

Study and implementation of advanced volatility models for portfolio optimization. The project combines econometric models such as ARCH/GARCH, MGARCH and HAR-RV with neural approaches including MLP and LSTM models for forecasting returns and covariance matrices. Portfolio construction is performed through constrained optimization and risk-adjusted objectives.
